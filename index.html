// YOUR PROPERTY

Different properties. Different requirements.

The right system depends on who uses your building, how access is managed and what is already installed.

Apartment buildings & strata

Visitor entry, shared doors and intercom upgrades for buildings with multiple residents. We assess existing cabling and equipment to identify a suitable upgrade or repair approach.

Businesses & commercial properties

CCTV and controlled entry for offices, shops and other business premises. Discuss who needs access, what areas need coverage and how the system will be used each day.

Homes & townhouses

See who is at the door, manage entry and monitor key areas of your property. We help you choose a system that suits your layout and day-to-day needs.

Existing systems & older buildings

An unreliable system does not always need a complete replacement. Fault finding and an assessment of existing equipment can help establish what can be repaired, retained or upgraded.
